# 🌟 ENGLISH_ASSIT - AI English Learning & Exam Preparation Platform

ENGLISH_ASSIT is a state-of-the-art, interactive AI-powered English learning ecosystem designed to accelerate fluency, vocabulary acquisition, grammar accuracy, essay writing, and standardized exam preparation (IELTS & TOEFL).

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Key Features

### 1. 📊 Interactive Learning Analytics Dashboard
- **Daily Streak & XP Gamification**: Track daily learning consistency, study streaks, and XP points.
- **CEFR Level Progression**: Real-time evaluation badge from **A1 (Beginner)** to **C2 (Mastery)**.
- **Skill Radar & Performance Metrics**: Visual progress across 6 key pillars: *Speaking, Listening, Reading, Writing, Grammar, Vocabulary*.
- **Daily Practice Recommendations**: Automated daily challenge recommendations tailored to learner level.

### 2. 💬 AI Voice & Chat Conversation Tutor
- **Multi-Persona AI Tutors**: Switch between *Native Friend*, *Patient Teacher*, and *Strict IELTS Examiner*.
- **Contextual Scenarios**: Job Interview, Coffee Shop Chat, Airport & Travel, Business Negotiation, IELTS Speaking Parts 1-3.
- **Real-Time Speech Synthesis & Recognition**: Native audio pronunciation (US, UK, AU accents) and microphone speech-to-text.
- **Instant Inline Feedback**: Live grammar corrections, vocabulary upgrades, and fluency feedback.

### 3. 📚 Grammar & Vocabulary Mastery Center
- **CEFR Flashcards with Spaced Repetition**: Interactive 3D flip cards (A1-C2) with audio pronunciation, example sentences, and Vietnamese translations.
- **Sentence Syntax Analyzer**: Color-coded grammatical analysis highlighting clauses, tenses, prepositions, and part-of-speech tags.
- **English Word Chain Game**: Interactive mini-game for vocabulary building with combo scoring, timer, and letter constraints.

### 4. ✍️ AI Writing & Essay Evaluator
- **IELTS / TOEFL Essay Grading**: Band Score calculator (0.0 - 9.0) with criterion scoring (*Task Achievement, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy*).
- **Side-by-Side Correction View**: Diff-style view showing original submission vs. AI optimized native version.
- **Topic Prompt Library**: IELTS Task 1 & Task 2 prompts covering opinion essays, discussion, problem-solution, and graph reports.

### 5. 🇻🇳 ➔ 🇬🇧 Vietnamese-to-English Translation Trainer (Luyện Dịch Việt - Anh)
- **Multi-Level Translation Prompts**: Curated Vietnamese practice sentences categorized by level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
- **Instant AI Scoring & Feedback**: Semantic accuracy score (0-100%), alternative native phrasings, and key vocabulary highlights.

### 6. 🎙️ Standardized Exam Simulator (IELTS & TOEFL)
- **Timed IELTS Speaking Module**:
  - *Part 1*: Warm-up general questions (4-5 mins).
  - *Part 2*: Cue Card with 1-minute prep timer and 2-minute speaking clock.
  - *Part 3*: Two-way deep analytical discussion.
- **Fluency & Pronunciation Metrics**: Filler word counter (`um`, `like`, `you know`), speaking speed (WPM), and vocabulary richness rating.

### 7. ⚙️ Settings & Personalization
- **Voice Accent & Speed Tuning**: Select preferred voice (US English, UK English, AU English) and playback rate (0.75x - 1.25x).
- **Target CEFR Level**: Set target milestones (B1, B2, C1, C2).
- **LocalStorage Data Persistence**: Save all learning stats, custom saved words, chat logs, and exam results locally.

---

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture & Stack

- **Core Framework**: React 18 (Vite)
- **UI & Layout**: Tailwind CSS v3 / Modern Vanilla CSS Design System with Glassmorphism, Neon Accents & Dynamic Micro-Animations
- **Iconography**: Lucide React Icons (`lucide-react`)
- **Audio & Speech Engine**: Native Web Speech API (`SpeechSynthesis` & `SpeechRecognition`)
- **Data Visualization**: Recharts / SVG Interactive Charts
- **State & Storage**: React Context + LocalStorage Persistence
